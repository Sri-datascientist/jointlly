What is Jointlly Real Estate Marketplace?

Jointlly is India's premier verified decision and matchmaking platform connecting property landowners, accredited construction companies, and structural engineering professionals. By standardizing due diligence and legal verification, Jointlly unlocks transparent joint venture agreements and contract construction partnerships across urban hubs like Bengaluru.

"According to industry real estate development benchmarks, transparent verification of legal titles and builder delivery track records increases project completion rates by over 35%."

On the other hand, traditional real estate negotiations often suffer from opaque land titles, delayed builder timelines, and unverified contractor credentials. Jointlly resolves these challenges through algorithmic title checking and audited developer portfolios.

How to Get Started on Jointlly: Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Submit Land or Project Details: Landowners or developers register property location, zoning details, and target development goals.
  2. Algorithmic & Legal Verification: Jointlly's dual-engine checks land titles, encumbrance certificates, RERA registrations, and developer track records.
  3. Smart Matchmaking: The platform matches land parcels with builders matching specific financial, architectural, and geographic capabilities.
  4. Execute Transparent Agreements: Finalize Joint Venture (JV) ratio or contract construction milestones with standardized legal templates.
  5. Milestone Escrow & Quality Audits: Track foundation, structure, and finishing stages with verified reporting.
